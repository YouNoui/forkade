# Forkade community code of conduct

Everyone is welcome to play, report problems and contribute. Be kind, explain disagreements respectfully, and help people learn. Give credit for others' work and respect their licenses.

Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, post sexual content, publish private information, impersonate others, or spam the community. Do not submit copied games or use the project to track players. Keep contributions suitable for all ages.

These expectations apply to this repository, issues, pull requests, public rankings and project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, reject contributions, warn participants, or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate and consider context.

Report a concern privately to **hello@iamynoui.com**. Include a link and a short description; avoid sharing unnecessary personal information. Reports will be handled discreetly and shared only with people needed to investigate. If a maintainer is involved, say so in the report. You may appeal a decision at the same address.

This is Forkade's own community policy, maintained by IAMYNOUI.
