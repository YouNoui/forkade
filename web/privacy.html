Forkade Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-14-v1 · Last updated 14 Nov 2026 · English is the official version.
Plain-language product draft. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

Forkade (playforkade.com) is operated by IAMYNOUI, a sole proprietorship registered in Singapore (UEN 53535234D). Contact: hello@iamynoui.com. Short version: no accounts, no ads, no tracking cookies, and your progress stays on your device.

Data Protection Officer

Younes Noui, Data Protection Officer, IAMYNOUI — hello@iamynoui.com. We respond to requests for access, correction or deletion within 30 days.

What stays on your device

High scores, cores, badges, skins, your streak, the nickname box, Playground drafts and a random player ID are saved in your browser's local storage. We can't see any of it. Clearing your browser's site data, or the button on the Delete my data page, removes it.

Anonymous counts

To see which games people enjoy, we count visits and plays per game per day. To avoid counting the same visitor twice in a day, a hash of your network address and browser type, salted with a value that changes daily, is added to an approximate counter that cannot be turned back into an address. Raw network addresses are not stored, no cookies are set, and nothing is linked to you. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing is counted.

Rankings (only if you tap Post)

When you post a score we store your nickname, the game, your best score and your random player ID. Your network address is used briefly, as a hash, to stop spam and is deleted after 60 seconds. Rankings are public, so please don't use your real full name as a nickname.

Who handles it

Vercel hosts the site and runs the rankings and counting functions. Upstash stores the rankings and daily counts. GitHub hosts the open-source code and any game you choose to submit there. Each gets only what it needs. Data may be processed outside Singapore with contractual and technical safeguards.

Cookies and ads

Forkade sets no cookies and shows no ads. We never sell data and never share it for advertising.

Retention

Daily counts are kept for about 13 months. Weekly boards expire after 3 weeks. All-time scores stay until you remove them or we remove them for breaking the rules. Spam-check hashes are deleted after 60 seconds.

Your rights

Singapore's PDPA is our baseline. Where EU or UK law applies you may also request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object, and may complain to your local regulator. Remove your rankings data instantly with Remove my scores in Rankings, or email hello@iamynoui.com with your player ID from the Delete my data page.

Children

Forkade is made to be played by everyone. Players under 13 should ask a parent before posting a nickname to the rankings.

AI training

We do not use anything you post or submit to train AI models, and we do not give it to anyone else to train AI models.
