Forkade Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-24-v12 · Last updated 24 Nov 2026 · English is the official version.
Plain-language product draft. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

Forkade (playforkade.com) is operated by IAMYNOUI, a sole proprietorship registered in Singapore (UEN 53535234D). Contact: hello@iamynoui.com. Short version: no accounts, no ads, no tracking cookies, and your progress stays on your device.

Data Protection Officer

Younes Noui, Data Protection Officer, IAMYNOUI — hello@iamynoui.com. We respond to requests for access, correction or deletion within 30 days.

What stays on your device

Settings, daily rewards, high scores, cores, badges, skins, your streak, the nickname box, Playground drafts and a random player ID are saved in your browser's local storage. We can't see any of it. Clearing your browser's site data, or the button on the Delete my data page, removes it.

Echo Rumble beta

Your avatar and best score are saved only in this browser. Echo squad input replays exist only in memory during a run and are discarded when you leave the game. Only explicit Post shares a validated input replay, preset nickname and avatar look for three days. Forge sharing uses closed presets. No purchases or free-text names.

Anonymous counts

An anonymous A/B group letter is sent with visit/play counts to test small layout choices. The letter and first-counted date stay on your device; first-day/next-day return flags let us estimate day-one returns without sending a device ID. Rules, rewards, odds and levels are identical in both groups. Anonymous visit and play counts are on by default. You can switch them off in Settings. To see which games people enjoy, we count visits and plays per game per day. To avoid counting the same visitor twice in a day, a hash of your network address and browser type, salted with a value that changes daily, is added to an approximate counter that cannot be turned back into an address. Raw network addresses are not stored, no cookies are set, and nothing is linked to you. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing is counted.

Rankings (only if you tap Post)

When a round starts online, we store a random token with only the game and start time (no player ID, name or contact details) for at most two hours, so a posted score can be checked against the time played. Only when you tap Post do we store your nickname, the game, your best score and your random player ID. Your network address is used briefly, as a hash, to stop spam and is deleted after 60 seconds. Rankings are public, so please don't use your real full name as a nickname. If you report a nickname, your random player ID is kept with that report for up to 60 days, only so the same person can't report someone twice.

Rumble replays and Forge

Only if you tap Post, Rumble stores your anonymous input replay, preset nickname and avatar look with your score for up to three days; other players can race that posted ghost. Rumble 2 additionally stores the selected hero, difficulty, abilities and upgrade choices needed to validate the replay. Before Post, its anonymous two-hour run token binds the selected hero, difficulty, weekly mode and equipped gadget; it contains no player ID. All ranked games bind a server-issued seed to their single-use run token. Local XP, mastery and the cosmetics collection are not sent. Friend links expose only the same explicitly posted ghost and expire with it after three days. Private live co-op is not enabled: there are no rooms, peer-to-peer connections, voice or free-text chat. We replay the inputs to check the score, but cannot guarantee every player is honest. Only Publish shares a Forge preset configuration, a preset name and your random device key for up to 90 days. No typed names, contacts or executable game code are accepted. Weekly Forge play counts increase after a run has started for at least ten seconds, when anonymous counting is enabled; they are play counts, not unique people, and expire after two weeks. Reports retain a random reporter device key for up to 60 days to prevent duplicate reports.

Promo codes (only if you redeem)

A redemption stores the code and a salted hash of your random device ID, to prevent repeat use, until the owner deletes that code. It stores no name or raw IP address. A separately salted network-address hash limits attempts to 10 per minute and expires after 60 seconds. A separate per-code, per-day network hash limits successful claims to five and expires within 24 hours. Clearing storage does not reset this network limit. Cores and cosmetic rewards stay on your device; they never change scores, rankings or badges. Clearing local storage removes your rewards and device key.

Who handles it

Vercel hosts the site and runs the rankings and counting functions. Upstash stores the rankings and daily counts. GitHub hosts the open-source code and any game you choose to submit there. Each gets only what it needs. Data may be processed outside Singapore with contractual and technical safeguards.

Cookies and ads

Forkade sets no cookies and shows no ads. We never sell data and never share it for advertising.

Retention

Daily counts are kept for about 13 months. Run tokens expire in two hours and are consumed on submission. Daily boards and pending scores expire after three days. Weekly boards expire after 3 weeks. All-time scores stay until you remove them or we remove them for breaking the rules. Most spam-check hashes expire after 60 seconds. Wrong owner-key attempts use a salted network hash for ten minutes; after five failures, further wrong attempts are blocked until that window ends. Promo daily network limits expire within 24 hours. Moderation reasons and decision sources are kept in a capped history (100 decisions, up to 90 days); the affected player can see a notice for 60 days.

Your rights

Singapore's PDPA is our baseline. Where EU or UK law applies you may also request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object, and may complain to your local regulator. Remove your rankings data instantly with Remove my scores in Rankings, or email hello@iamynoui.com with your player ID from the Delete my data page.

Children

Forkade is made to be played by everyone. Players under 13 should ask a parent before posting a nickname to the rankings.

AI training

We do not use anything you post or submit to train AI models, and we do not give it to anyone else to train AI models.
