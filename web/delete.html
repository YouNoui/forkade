Forkade has no accounts, so you are in control of your data. Here is how to remove each part.

Rankings: instant

Open Rankings on the home screen and tap Remove my scores and nickname. It deletes your nickname and every score you posted, on every board, straight away.

Rankings: by email

Can't use this device any more? Email hello@iamynoui.com with your player ID (shown below on the device you played on) and we will remove it within 30 days.

Progress on this device

Your scores, cores, badges, skins and Playground draft live only in this browser. The button below clears them. This cannot be undone.

What we keep

Anonymous daily counts of visits and plays are not linked to you or your device, so there is nothing in them to delete.
