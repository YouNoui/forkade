COMMUNITY RULES

Play fair, keep it friendly

Version 2026-11-14-v1 · Last updated 14 Nov 2026 · English is the official version.
Plain-language product draft. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

These rules keep the rankings and submitted games fun and safe for everyone, including younger players.

Nicknames

2–14 letters or numbers. No real full names, no rude, hateful or sexual words, no pretending to be someone else, and no ads or links.

Fair play

Rewind is part of the game, so use it as much as you like. Don't post edited or scripted scores, don't use bots, and don't flood the rankings.

Submitted games

Make it your own. Genres are free to use, but don't copy another game's name, characters, art, piece set or exact layout, and don't use brand names. No tracking, network calls or ads, and keep it suitable for all ages.

Moderation

We review reports sent to hello@iamynoui.com and may remove scores, nicknames or games that break these rules. There are no automatic bans; decisions consider context and evidence.
