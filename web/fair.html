FAIR PLAY

Same rules for everyone.

Lots of free games quietly change the rules on you. FORKADE doesn't. Every number below is read live from the game's own code, so this page can't say one thing while the game does another. The code is open: check it yourself.

What FORKADE never does

  • No hidden difficulty. Levels don't quietly get easier or harder depending on how often you lose. Random levels use plain randomness, and Demolition Chain only deals levels it has checked can be solved.
  • Disclosed prize machine. Optional spins use local cores only. Chances are shown before spinning; duplicate refunds and the re-roll guarantee are listed below.
  • No ads and no paid boosts. Cores can't be bought, and no game is easier for people who pay.
  • No secret tests. Everyone gets the same rules, rewards and levels. No A/B tests, no player segments.
  • Daily round bonuses are limited. The full, half and quarter rates below apply only to round cores, never to other rewards.
  • No made-up numbers. The cores you see after a round are exactly what you earned, line by line.
  • No phone details. FORKADE doesn't read your phone model, screen brightness, internet speed, photos or contacts. The only thing sent is an anonymous “visit” or “play” count (you can switch it off in Settings) and scores you choose to Post. Privacy
